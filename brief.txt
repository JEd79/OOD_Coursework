CSF2702: Object Oriented Design – Coursework
Due: 14:00am, 21st October 2026
You are part of a software development team that is creating a system for South Wales Hire and Haulage
(SWHH), a vehicle hire and haulage company. Your team has already carried out a requirements analysis
and now it is up to you to create a class diagram that models the classes and relationships between classes
of the SWHH’s hire system. The class diagram must have a fairly low level of abstraction (it should be
detailed) and should contain enough information that it could be given to a programmer to implement.
You do NOT need to produce any code for this coursework.
There are 25 marks available for this coursework and overall the coursework is worth 15% of the module.
An Important Note
Make sure that your model sticks to the problem as it is deﬁned below. It can be tempting to think
ahead and imagine extra or useful features in the system but please do not include these in your class
diagram, you will not be graded for any additional or unnnessisary additions. Try to stick to the business
processes involved.
Essential Functionaliy
[20 marks]
Below are the requirements that you will need to work from. The systems needs to store information on
customers, vehicles and rentals.
1. Each customer has various details associated with them such as names, contact information and
drivers licence information.
2. A drivers license has a number, an expiry date, number of years held and categories of vehicles
that can be driven.
3. The company is quite large and therefore hires out a variety of haulage vehicles. The three types
of vehicle they hire are:
• Articulated Lorries.
• Luton Vans.
• Standard Vans.
4. All vehicles have a fuel type, left or righthand drive and a gearbox type (automatic/manual), a
maximum payload weight and a height. Standard Vans have the option of a tow bar attachment,
Luton Vans have the option of a tail lift, and Articulated Lorries can optionally be hired out with
a trailer.
5. The system should be able to add customers, remove customers, add vehicles, remove vehicles and
produce a list of rentals that begin or end on a given date.
6. The system should be able to search for available vehicles by specifying the number of seats, the
make or model or the available storage space (in m3).
7. The system should be able to create a rental with the following information.
• The vehicle that is being rented and its current mileage.
• The date and time the rental begins.
• How many days the rental is for.

Damage Reports
[5 marks]
The company regularly deals with vehicles being returned with some damage and this can lead to disputes
with the customers. They would like to enforce that every rental has at least two damage reports; one
at the beginning of the rental and one at the end of a rental. They would like to store information on
any exterior damage, interior damage and engine problems as textual descriptions. They would also like
to record whether the damage needs to be ﬁxed and a customer signature.
Customers should be able to check the damage reports associated with their rental and the company
would like to be able to produce a list of damage reports that need ﬁxing.
Marks will be awarded for a good, practical design that could be implemented.
